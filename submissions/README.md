# Submissions

Competition closed - evaluated on a held-out split.

The Kaggle CAFA 6 Protein Function Prediction competition is closed and does not accept late submissions (nor are test ground-truth labels publicly available). In accordance with the CENG313 project guidelines, Assignment 1 models are evaluated on a 20% held-out split of the training dataset (`test_size=0.20, random_state=42`) and compared against the competition leaderboard (`0.52430` 1st place) and public baseline notebooks in `assignment1_eda_baseline.ipynb` (Section 7).
